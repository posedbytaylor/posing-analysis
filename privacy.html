Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

This notice explains what information I collect when you sign up for a free posing analysis, what I do with it, and your rights. I've kept it as simple as I can, but if you have any questions, just email me.

Who I am

I'm Ellie Taylor, trading as Posed by Taylor, a posing coach based in Leeds, West Yorkshire. I'm responsible for the information you share with me. You can contact me at posedbytaylor@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • From the sign-up form: your first name, email address, competition category, show date and Instagram handle.
  • On WhatsApp: your phone number, your messages, and the posing photos you send me.

What I use it for

  • To give you your free posing analysis. I use your photos and details to record a personal video breaking down your posing, tailored to your category and show date.
  • To tell you about my posing coaching. At the end of your video, I'll talk you through my coaching packages.
  • To send you emails. When you sign up, you'll also get posing tips, updates and offers from me by email. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by emailing me.

Who helps me store and send it

I use a few trusted services to run this, and your information is stored with them:

  • Brevo: stores your sign-up details and sends my emails.
  • Netlify: hosts this website.
  • WhatsApp: where you send me your photos and we chat.
  • Loom: where I record and share your video analysis.

I never sell your information, and I don't share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

  • Your original photos are deleted once I've sent you your posing analysis.
  • Your video analysis stays on Loom so you can rewatch it. Ask me any time and I'll delete it.
  • Your sign-up details stay on my email list until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them.

Sharing on social media

I may share screenshots from video analyses on my social media to show how I coach, and these could show your photos on screen. I'll always ask your permission first, and I will never share your original photos.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • send you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop sending you emails

Just email posedbytaylor@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
